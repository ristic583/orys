Logo directions

Six different ideas in the current colours. Each is shown large, next to the name as in the top bar, and at browser-tab size.

1Three loops

The product's three loops (architect, plan, build) interlocked; the build loop in lime.

2Always moving

Steps that keep going forward on their own; the newest step in lime. Very readable when small.

3Orbit

A worker circling the project and coming back to where it stopped. Round, calm, like the current pie.

4Lettermark

A bold black-and-white "ip" with a violet dot on the i. Typographic and confident; reads like a brand, not an icon.

5Progress

Three phases growing to done, violet to lime on black. Matches the big-number, chart-heavy dashboard.

6Route P

A "P" drawn as a route that loops back on itself, with a lime stop where the work is now.
